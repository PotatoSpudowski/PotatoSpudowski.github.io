*This isn't a portfolio. Its a narrative.*

If you want the sanitized version of my career, go to LinkedIn. If you want a one page summary optimized for ATS bots, my resume is floating around somwhere. This site exists because neither of those let me say what I actually want to say.

Resumes are basically a lossy compression format for human experience. You take something messy and interesting, like spending a weekend building a prediction model for oil well efficiency because you thought it was cool, and you compress it into a bullet point that says "Developed ML model for energy sector optimization." Nobody reads that and thinks this person is interesting. They just think this person knows the right keywords. LinkedIn is even worse honestly. Everyone on there is "leveraging cross-functional synergies" and "passionate about driving impact." You cant tell the difference between someone who actually built something from scratch and someone who attended a meeting about it.

So this is my version of things. The timeline on the home page is the core of it. I just write what I did in my own words, how I'd explain it to someone over coffee. Some entries are jobs, some are side projects, some are hackathon things that went nowhere but taught me something. When you scroll through and see the density of stuff I've done, that tells you way more about me than any resume could. Not because of any single line item but because of the pattern.

Its also just nice to have a running log of everything for myself. Its easy to forget the things you've done when your always focused on the next thing. And if your thinking about working with me or collaborating on something, just scroll through. You'll get it.
